# Big C's Fishing Co. — Amazon PPC Strategy

## Strategy Overview
1. **Auto Campaigns:** Launch 1 Auto campaign for every product to mine Amazon's data.
2. **Keyword Mining:** After 2 weeks, pull the 'Search Term Report' and identify winners.
3. **Manual Campaigns:** Move winners to Manual Exact Match campaigns.
4. **Competitor Targeting:** Target big brands (PowerPro, Berkley) using ASIN targeting.

## Jungle Scout Import
I have generated a clean CSV for Jungle Scout: `JungleScout_Keywords_Import.csv`.
Use the 'Keyword Scout' or 'Ads' tool in Jungle Scout to upload these terms.

## Budget & Bidding
- **Daily Budget:** Start at $10/day per product.
- **Initial Bid:** $0.50 - $0.85 (adjust based on impressions).
